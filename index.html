Historic Regulatory Shift

The 2026 Non-Saudi Real Estate Ownership Law

Effective from January 2026, the landmark Real Estate Ownership Law enables foreign nationals, corporations, and institutional investors to acquire property rights under regulated, secure geographic zones.

Saudi Premium Residency (Golden Visa)

Property owners qualifying through residential holdings can obtain multi-year or permanent residency with rights for the investor, spouse, parents, and children under 25.

Exemption from Expatriate Levies

Holders of the property-linked Premium Residency are exempt from monthly dependent and companion fees levied on foreign residents.

Privileged Fast-Track Privileges

Access to citizen & GCC lanes at international airports, alongside uninhibited freedom to work and invest in the Saudi private sector.
